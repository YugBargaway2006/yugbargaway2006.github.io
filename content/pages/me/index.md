Hey, I'm Tania! Welcome to my spot on the web for [writing](/blog), [projects](/projects), and art! I'm currently working as a principal software engineer at Balto AI, and in my free time I like working out, biking, playing video games, and reading. I spend most of my time with my partner and our three cats.

This site is a space for self-expression and to share what I've learned with the world, and has always had **no ads, no affiliate links, no tracking or analytics, no sponsored posts, and no paywall**.

I hope I can inspire others to make their own creative corner on the web even against the tsunami of [enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) on the internet.

If you want to stay up to date with my posts, sign up for the [newsletter](https://taniarascia.substack.com) or subscribe to the [RSS feed](/rss.xml). There's over a decade of articles and open-source code to peruse through, so please enjoy!
