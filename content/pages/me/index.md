Hey, I'm Yug! Welcome to my spot on the web for [writing](/blog), [projects](/projects), and art! I'm currently studying Computer Science for my graduate degree at Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IITKGP), India, and will be working as an intern software engineer at Capital One on summer 2027. In my free time I like going out to the nature, biking, playing video games and exploring new stuff apart from technology. I spend most of my time alone, and would appreciate a companion in my journey.

This site is a space for self-expression and to share what I've learned with the world, and has always had **no ads, no affiliate links, no tracking or analytics, no sponsored posts, and no paywall**.

I hope I can inspire others to make their own creative corner on the web even against the tsunami of [enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) on the internet.

If you want to stay up to date with my posts, I am planning to have a newsletter, if I get enough time and effort to maintain it. Till then, you can subscribe to the [RSS feed](/rss.xml). There shall be a decade long journey of updating this website with my life experiences, so please enjoy!
